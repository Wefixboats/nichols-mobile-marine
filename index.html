FAQ

Do you really come to my boat?
Yes — that's the entire business. Dock, marina, boat ramp, or your driveway. Wherever the boat sits, that's my shop for the day.
What does the $199 diagnostic cover?
The trip to your boat plus a full diagnostic evaluation, within 30 miles. If you approve the repair, labor is $150/hr and parts are at cost.
What engines do you work on?
Mercury, Yamaha, Suzuki, Evinrude/Johnson, and Tohatsu outboards (including older OMC two-strokes); MerCruiser and Volvo Penta sterndrives; PCM inboards; and jet skis.
How fast can you get to me?
Call or text and I'll give you a straight answer. If I'm booked out, I'll tell you — I won't leave you guessing.
